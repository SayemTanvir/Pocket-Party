# Pocket Party — Public Beta

Short description: Five quick games for friends. Play locally, challenge a bot, or join a private online room.

## Description to paste into itch.io

**Five games. One party.**

Pick a challenge, bring a friend, and play another round.

- **Dots & Boxes:** claim boxes on a large board and turn one line into a comeback.
- **Chess:** classic chess with optional clocks.
- **Darts:** the aim sweeps across the dartboard. Time your throw to hit the big scores.
- **Word Grid:** take turns placing letters. Complete new 3–5 letter English words across or down to score their length. Every distinct word scores once per match.
- **Connect Four:** drop a disc, build a line, and block your opponent.

Play against a bot, share one device, or create a private online room and send the code to a friend. Dots & Boxes, Darts and Word Grid support 2–4 players; Chess and Connect Four support 2. Bot matches are one player against one bot.

### Controls

Use a mouse or touch. Each game has a **How to play** button. In Word Grid, tap an empty tile to choose a letter. In Darts, tap the board or **Throw dart** when the moving marker reaches your target.

### Playing online

Choose a game, select **Play with friends online**, and create a room. Your friends join with the same room code. The first connection may take about a minute while the free server wakes up.

### Public beta

This is an early version, and feedback is welcome in the comments. Please include the game, device/browser and what happened.

- Online rooms are temporary. A server restart can end a match.
- Win/loss counts last for the current match series; they are not saved to an account.
- Bots are casual opponents, and online competitive anti-cheat has not been implemented.
- No iOS app download is included. Browser compatibility is still being tested.

### Android download

The optional Android APK is a release-signed beta. Download it on an Android device and allow installation from the browser/file manager when Android asks. It is a separate installation from the earlier development build.
